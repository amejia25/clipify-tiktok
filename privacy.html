Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy describes the planned TikTok integration for the independently configured Clipify AI desktop application.

Information accessed

With your authorization, the integration may access TikTok account identifiers and basic profile information, and submit videos, captions, and publishing metadata to TikTok.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your TikTok account, perform requested video uploads, and report publishing results.

Storage and security

Authentication credentials should be stored securely and must not be published in public repositories. The integration is intended for use on an authorized desktop computer.

Third-party services

Video publishing uses TikTok's APIs and is subject to TikTok's privacy practices.

Data retention and deletion

Users may revoke the integration's TikTok access through their TikTok account settings. Locally stored authorization credentials can also be deleted to disconnect the integration.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the repository owner through the project's GitHub Issues page.
